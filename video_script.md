## 🎬 Tips for Recording Your Demo Video:
1. **Resolution:** Record at 1080p (1920x1080) or 720p with browser zoom at 90% or 100% so all panels and map controls are crisp.
2. **Audio:** Use a clear microphone or headset with minimal background noise.
3. **Pacing:** Move your mouse smoothly — do not jerk or shake the cursor. Hover intentionally over the numbers or buttons being mentioned in the voiceover.
4. **Pre-caching:** Both backend (`http://localhost:8000`) and frontend (`http://localhost:3000`) are running live right now, so clicks will respond instantly.
